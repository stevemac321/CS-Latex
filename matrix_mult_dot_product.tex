\textbf{Raw Matrices} A \text{ and } B:

\[
A = 
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\quad
B = 
\begin{bmatrix}
5 & 6 \\
7 & 8
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Step-by-Step Multiplication:}

\[
C = 
\begin{bmatrix}
(1 \times 5 + 2 \times 7) & (1 \times 6 + 2 \times 8) \\
(3 \times 5 + 4 \times 7) & (3 \times 6 + 4 \times 8)
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Interpretation with Matrix Indexing:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \( C[0,0] = A[\text{row 0}] \times B[\text{col 0}] = 1 \times 5 + 2 \times 7 = 19 \)
    \item \( C[0,1] = A[\text{row 0}] \times B[\text{col 1}] = 1 \times 6 + 2 \times 8 = 22 \)
    \item \( C[1,0] = A[\text{row 1}] \times B[\text{col 0}] = 3 \times 5 + 4 \times 7 = 43 \)
    \item \( C[1,1] = A[\text{row 1}] \times B[\text{col 1}] = 3 \times 6 + 4 \times 8 = 50 \)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Final Matrix} C:

\[
C = 
\begin{bmatrix}
19 & 22 \\
43 & 50
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Summation for Each Entry of } C:

\[
C[i,j] = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} A[i,k] \times B[k,j]
\]

\textbf{Dot Product Definition:}

The dot product of two vectors \( \mathbf{a} \) and \( \mathbf{b} \) of the same size is defined as:

\[
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i b_i
\]

where \( a_i \) and \( b_i \) are the components of vectors \( \mathbf{a} \) and \( \mathbf{b} \), respectively, and \( n \) is the number of components in the vectors.

This definition of dot product is what each entry of matrix \( C \) represents: the dot product of a row from matrix \( A \) and a column from matrix \( B \).
